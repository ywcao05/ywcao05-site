Videos

These are some short mathematical videos made using the Python package Manim during a department summer internship.

These two videos are about map projections. The first video is designed for general audience and explains the basic problem of representing the curved surface of the Earth on a flat map and introduces several common projections and their distortions. The second video is more mathematical and uses complex analysis to show that the Mercator projection is conformal.
